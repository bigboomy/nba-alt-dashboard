How to read this

Alt: the stepped-down OVER line chosen from the book's ladder. Hit%: how often the player cleared it over a recent sample (shown as hits/games). Edge: hit% minus the book's implied probability (1 ÷ odds), in percentage points; a pick must be at least +5. Stars are capped early in the season because samples are small. Opp is the opponent's allowance of that stat per 100 possessions and its league rank; it is shown for context and never changes a pick. Projected totals are a pace-and-rating model shown for interest only.

Not betting advice. Hit rate is not profit; prices move and players sit. Past results do not predict future ones. Only bet what you can afford to lose. If gambling is a problem for you or someone you know, contact your local support service (Australia: 1800 858 858, gamblinghelponline.org.au).
